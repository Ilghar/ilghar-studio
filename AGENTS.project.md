# ilghar.studio — project instructions

## Visual system (covers & carousels)

Document covers live on the **left page of the first spread**. Crop the **top square** of that left page (title + geometric mark). Do not invent a subject from the right-hand certificate.

- Abschluss / Arbeits / Fach / Werk: designed geometric cover, left page, top square.
- CV: left page, top square (the Lebenslauf itself).
- BLADE: use the supplied square JPEG as-is. No recrop.
- Studio: the purple sculpture PNG (`ilghar.studio` lettering).
- Notes card: inner still-life square, not the polaroid frame.
- Portfolio 2023: A4 cover page, top square around the star.

Studio notes carousel is a **continuous strip**: no gap, no rounded frame, pages abut. Other documents keep framed slides.

Rasterize PDFs at high scale (≈2.5×) with JPEG 4:4:4. Never down-res the original notes JPEGs.
